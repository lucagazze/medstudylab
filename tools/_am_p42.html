Toxicity & Safety

Toxicity refers to the capacity of a substance to cause cellular injury and adverse pathological effects in the organism.

THERAPEUTIC WINDOW (MARGIN OF SAFETY)

A drug's safety profile depends on the span between effective and toxic concentrations.

Wide Window (Safe)

Large margin between the therapeutic dose and the toxic threshold. Extremely difficult to overdose accidentally (e.g. Penicillin, Amoxicillin).

Narrow Window (High Risk)

Effective dose is dangerously close to toxic concentration. Requires strict therapeutic drug monitoring (e.g. Warfarin, Digoxin, Lithium).

MECHANISMS OF TOXICITY
Dose-Dependent Toxicity: Most toxic reactions are directly related to quantity. High doses saturate hepatic metabolic enzymes and renal clearance mechanisms, causing accumulation and tissue injury.
